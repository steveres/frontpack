Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version

  • We collect your name and email to give you an account, and we store the photos and videos you upload.
  • We use your data only to run FrontPack. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Your files can contain the date and GPS location where they were taken. Anyone you share a trip with can see those.
  • You can delete your files, your trips or your whole account at any time.

1. Who we are

FrontPack is run by FrontPack, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for your personal information. If you have a question or request, contact steve@thefrontpack.com. If another company takes over running FrontPack, for example a change of the company's legal form, your information passes to it under this policy.

2. What we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we receive your name, your email address and an account ID. They come from you or from the sign-in service you choose: email, Google or Apple. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive an Apple relay address instead. We never see your password.

Your content

This is what you upload and create: photos, videos, trip names and descriptions, your choice of music from our library, and your sharing settings. Photo and video files often contain extra information. That can include the date and time they were taken, the GPS location, and the camera or phone model. We read the date and location to build your slideshow and map.

Technical information

When you use the site, our servers receive your IP address, your browser type and the pages and files requested. We use this to keep the Service working and secure, for example to limit abuse. Microsoft Azure keeps our diagnostic and operational logs for us, for up to 30 days.

Stored in your browser

The site stores a few items in your browser: your sign-in session, so you stay signed in, and your slideshow preferences, such as speed and music volume. These are needed for the site to work. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or advertising or behavioral analytics services.

3. How we use it

  • To give you an account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your content, make previews and smaller versions, and play your slideshows with their map and timeline.
  • To show your trips to the people you share them with.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use your content to train AI models.

If you are in the European Union or the UK: we use your information to provide the Service you signed up for (performance of a contract). We also use it for our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure and working.

4. Who can see it

  • You. Your trips are private unless you share them.
  • People you share with. With a share link, anyone who has the link (including people it was passed on to, and without signing in) can watch that trip's slideshow. It shows the photos, videos, music and, if you allow it, the map. A public trip is listed for everyone on the Trip Pix page. Viewers can save and pass on what they see.
  • Our service providers, who handle data only to run FrontPack for us:
    • Microsoft Azure hosts the site and stores accounts and content in the United States. Microsoft Entra handles sign-in.
    • Google: maps are shown with Google Maps, which receives your IP address and the map area being shown. If you sign in with Google, Google also handles that sign-in. Google processes this under the Google Privacy Policy.
    • Apple, if you sign in with Apple.
  • When required. We may share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of users, the public or FrontPack.

5. Locations and dates: please read

Photo locations and dates can show where you live, where you are, and when you are away from home. When you share a trip, its map and the original files can reveal this, for you and for anyone pictured. Turning off Show the map to viewers hides the map, but it doesn't remove the location and date from the files: anyone who can see a shared trip can download the original photos and videos and read them. Before sharing, think about leaving out photos taken at home, turning off Show the map to viewers, or sharing after you return. On most phones, you can also turn off location tagging in the camera settings.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your content until you delete it, or until we remove it under our Terms of Use.
  • When you delete your account, from the Account page, we delete your trips, files and sign-in account. Leftover copies, such as logs, are deleted automatically, normally within 30 days.
  • We may keep a record of an account for longer if the law requires it, or if it was closed for abuse.

7. Your choices and rights

You can rename or delete trips, delete files, turn sharing on or off, and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or anything else, email steve@thefrontpack.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, such as the EU, the UK or certain U.S. states, the law may give you rights to:

  • access, correct, delete or move your information;
  • object to or limit how it is used;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

We respect these rights for everyone, wherever they live.

8. Security

Connections to FrontPack are encrypted (HTTPS). Content is stored encrypted, and access to our systems is limited. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that your information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

9. Age limit and children

FrontPack is only for people aged 21 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 21 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it. Photos of children may appear in trips only with a parent's or guardian's consent (see our Terms of Use).

10. International users

FrontPack is run from the United States, and your information is stored there. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is transferred to the U.S., where privacy laws may differ from yours.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the “Last updated” date, and for important changes we'll tell you on the site or by email.
