This page is for site admins. Ask the site owner for the Site admin role.

    Users

    Last seen is when they last used Trip Pix, to the hour. It's blank for anyone who hasn't used it since it started being recorded. Storage is their photos and videos as uploaded, against the quota.

    Trips

    Views are openings of the trip's share link (owners playing their own trips don't count). "Left out" photos and videos have no date or location: no slideshow shows them, View included. Opening a trip is logged.
