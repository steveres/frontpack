The FrontPack Story

Once Upon a Time

We had a cycling family growing up. Two adults, two kids, a sense of adventure and a sense of humor. I did my first long distance ride in college, leading a tour from Manhattan to Quebec City. The next big ride was with my girlfriend, now my wife, cycling from Eugene Oregon to San Francisco.

And Every Summer

As the kids grew, we went farther. From around the block to around the world. From days to weeks. From flat roads to climbs.

Until One Day

I was planning a multi-week cycling trip for the family and couldn't find an app that had the routing and tracking features I needed. So, I built an app for that. The trip was great, and the app got us from point A to point B together. The app, FrontPack, was like many you see today: a free web site to upload and download GPX, and a free mobile app for offline navigation.

And Because of That

Having succesfully used the app to navigate foreign lands, and with a small but growing online following, I offered Adventure Cycling Association the Frontpack technology. It provided them a way to distribute their iconic maps digitally, while in-app purchases fueled their mission. And it provided me a way to support the orgnization and bike touring community, while buying unnecessary bike equipment and taking the family on outrageous bike trips.

And Because of That

We evolved FrontPack into Bicycle Route Navigator and launched it in 2018. We updated it early and often, responding quickly to feedback. Cell phones and networks to evolved as well, so with more memory, and better batteries, and faster downloads, the challenges fell away. By 2024, FrontPack was used to plan and navigate over 25,000,000 miles .

Until One Day

As Adventure Cycling was developing their 5-year plan, they knew they needed broader digital distribution to reach a larger, more diverse audience. They chose to partner with RideWithGPS and we worked together to transition the routes from Bicycle Route Navigator to the new platform. It was the right decision for them, a great ride for me, and as they say, the rest is history.

And From That Day On

So, what's next? I'm not sure, but I do know that AI is changing how we work, learn, and play. It's unclear what that means for cycle touring but I'm going to find out.
